Tanda Bateri Phone Dah Rosak — Dan Apa Yang Perlu Anda BuatSigns Your Phone Battery Is Failing — And What To Do About It

Setiap bateri phone ada umur. Lepas guna setiap hari selama setahun dua, prestasi dia turun sikit-sikit sampai satu hari anda perasan phone dah tak boleh tahan sampai petang. Yang penting, anda kena tahu bila masalah tu memang bateri, dan bila sebenarnya benda lain — yang mungkin anda langsung tak payah bayar untuk baiki.

Tanda bateri anda dah lemah

  • Peratusan bateri jatuh mendadak. Dari 60% terus ke 20% dalam beberapa minit, atau tersangkut lama di satu nombor, lepas tu tiba-tiba jatuh.
  • Phone mati walaupun bateri belum habis. Tunjuk 30% tapi terus shut down — selalunya masa buka app berat, atau masa phone sejuk lepas lama dalam bilik aircond.
  • Panas luar biasa. Panas masa charge, atau panas walaupun anda tak buat apa-apa.
  • Charge makin lama, habis makin cepat. Atau sebaliknya — naik sampai 100% laju sangat, lepas tu turun pun sama laju.
  • Phone hidup hanya bila plug in. Cabut charger, terus mati.
  • Casing kembung, skrin terangkat, butang jadi ketat. Ini tanda bateri dah kembung.
  • Battery Health rendah. iPhone: Settings > Battery > Battery Health & Charging. Sesetengah Android ada maklumat sama dalam Settings atau app penjagaan bateri.

Kalau casing dah kembung, berhenti guna

Bateri kembung bukan benda yang boleh tunggu. Jangan tekan, jangan cucuk, jangan biar charging masa anda tidur atau keluar rumah, dan jangan sesekali cuba congkel keluar sendiri. Matikan phone, jauhkan dari tempat panas, dan bawa terus ke kedai. Daripada semua tanda di atas, ini satu-satunya yang kami minta anda jangan tunggu lama.

Cuba benda percuma ini dulu

Ramai yang sangka bateri dah rosak, rupa-rupanya bukan:

  • Tukar cable dan adapter. Cable dah koyak atau cable murah memang antara punca paling kerap. Pinjam cable kawan dan test.
  • Periksa charging port. Habuk dan serabut poket memang boleh buat charger tak duduk elok dalam port. Tengok dalam port dengan lampu suluh.
  • Tengok app mana yang makan bateri. Boleh tengok dalam Settings > Battery. Satu app yang bermasalah boleh habiskan bateri satu hari penuh.
  • Baru update software? Bagi masa dua tiga hari. Phone masih buat kerja di background, jadi bateri memang turun laju buat sementara.
  • Restart phone. Percuma, ambil masa seminit je, dan kadang-kadang tu je yang perlu.
  • Kawasan signal lemah. Phone asyik cari network sampai bateri habis, walaupun phone dalam poket je.

Kalau Battery Health masih tinggi dan phone jalan elok, anda tak perlu tukar bateri. Simpan duit tu dulu.

Cara panjangkan umur bateri

  • Panas memang pembunuh bateri nombor satu — jangan tinggal phone atas dashboard kereta masa panas terik.
  • Elak main game berat sambil fast charge.
  • Kalau boleh, kekalkan bateri sekitar 20% hingga 80% dan jangan selalu biar bateri habis sampai 0%.
  • Guna charger yang berkualiti, bukan yang paling murah kat rak kedai.

Bila memang kena tukar

Kalau simptom di atas dah ganggu kerja harian anda, tukar bateri selalunya jauh lebih berbaloi daripada beli phone baru. Backup data dulu — biasakan buat sebelum hantar mana-mana repair. Kalau phone masih dalam warranty dari kedai asal atau brand, check dengan mereka dahulu. Dan pastikan bateri gantian tu berkualiti — bateri murah selalunya bermasalah semula tak lama lepas tukar.

Every phone battery has a lifespan. After a year or two of daily use, performance drops bit by bit until one day you notice your phone can't make it to the evening any more. The important thing is knowing when the problem really is the battery, and when it's actually something else — something you may not need to pay to fix at all.

Signs your battery is going

  • The battery percentage drops in jumps. Straight from 60% to 20% in a few minutes, or stuck on one number for ages, then a sudden fall.
  • The phone dies before the battery is empty. It shows 30% and shuts down anyway — usually when you open a heavy app, or when the phone has gone cold after a long spell in an air-conditioned room.
  • Unusual heat. Hot while charging, or hot even when you're not doing anything.
  • Slower to charge, faster to drain. Or the opposite — races up to 100% and drops just as fast.
  • The phone only runs while plugged in. Unplug the charger and it dies.
  • Bulging casing, lifted screen, stiff buttons. That's a swollen battery.
  • Low Battery Health. On iPhone: Settings > Battery > Battery Health & Charging. Many Android phones show something similar in Settings or a battery care app.

If the casing is bulging, stop using it

A swollen battery isn't something you can wait on. Don't press it, don't puncture it, don't leave it charging while you're asleep or out of the house, and never try to pry it out yourself. Power the phone off, keep it away from heat, and bring it straight in. Of all the signs above, this is the one we'd ask you not to leave for later.

Try these free checks first

Plenty of "dead batteries" turn out to be nothing of the sort:

  • Swap the cable and adapter. A frayed cable or a cheap cable is one of the most common culprits. Borrow a friend's and test.
  • Check the charging port. Dust and pocket fluff really can stop the charger seating properly in the port. Shine a light inside and look.
  • See which app is eating the battery. You can check in Settings > Battery. One misbehaving app can drain a whole day's battery.
  • Just updated the software? Give it two or three days. The phone is still working in the background, so the battery genuinely drops faster for a while.
  • Restart the phone. Free, takes a minute, and sometimes that's all it needs.
  • Weak signal areas. The phone keeps hunting for network until the battery is gone, even sitting in your pocket.

If Battery Health is still high and the phone runs fine, you don't need a new battery. Keep your money for now.

How to make a battery last longer

  • Heat really is the number one battery killer — don't leave the phone on the car dashboard in the midday sun.
  • Avoid heavy gaming while fast charging.
  • Where you can, keep the charge roughly between 20% and 80% and don't let it run down to 0% too often.
  • Use a decent charger, not the cheapest one on the shop rack.

When it really does need replacing

If the symptoms above are getting in the way of your day, a battery replacement is usually far better value than a new phone. Back up your data first — make it a habit before you send in any repair. If the phone is still under warranty from the original seller or the brand, check with them first. And make sure the replacement battery is a quality one — cheap batteries usually give trouble again not long after the swap.
