Signs your charging port is failing

  • You have to press or twist the cable before the charging symbol appears
  • Charging cuts in and out on its own
  • Charging is very slow even with the original charger
  • The cable feels loose and falls out easily, even a brand new one
  • The port looks dusty, dirty, or has dark marks
  • The bottom of the phone gets hot while charging
  • Your laptop doesn't detect the charger at all and the charging light stays off
  • You've been surviving on wireless charging or a powerbank

Not every case needs a new port

Plenty of customers come in convinced the port is dead, but once we open up and inspect, the cause is something else. One of the most common causes is packed-in pocket dust and fabric fibres — the cable can't go all the way in, so the charge doesn't get through properly. In other cases it's the cable or the adapter that's faulty, not the phone. If that's all it is, we tell you straight. We don't replace parts that don't need replacing.

What we actually do

  • Free diagnostics — we check the port, cable, adapter and charging readings before touching anything
  • Professional port cleaning when the port is blocked with dust
  • Charging port / flex replacement for most iPhone and Android models
  • Board-level repair when the charging IC or the circuitry on the motherboard is the problem — fine soldering work, not just swapping a part
  • Laptop DC jacks and charging ports — bring it in for free diagnostics and we'll confirm whether your model can be repaired
  • Full retest before handover — charging, data transfer, and making sure the cable seats tightly in the port
