Signs your screen needs replacing

What most often comes through our door:

  • Cracked or shattered glass after a drop, even if the phone still looks fine
  • Touch not responding, or tapping by itself (ghost touch)
  • Green, purple or black lines across the screen
  • Screen stays black but the phone still vibrates, rings and takes calls
  • A dark ink-like blotch that keeps spreading
  • Screen lifting or crooked because the battery behind it has swollen
  • Laptop screen flickering, dim, or cracked from a loose hinge pressing the panel

What we actually do

We don't say "it needs a new screen" the moment you walk in. How we work:

  • Free diagnosis. We check and test first. Sometimes the fault isn't the screen at all — a loose flex cable, or something on the board. Then you save, because no screen replacement is needed.
  • A quote before any work starts. We explain what's broken, the part options, and the full price. You agree first, then we continue. No hidden charges.
  • The replacement itself. We take off the old panel, transfer gaskets and brackets properly, and seat the new screen so it doesn't lift again.
  • Testing before handover. Touch from edge to edge, screen brightness, earpiece, proximity sensor and Face ID (on models with Face ID) — all tested in front of you.

Every screen repair has a warranty. The period and coverage are explained at quoting time, before you agree — not after you've paid.

What affects the price

  • Model and year of the device. A screen for the newest iPhone doesn't cost the same as one from five years ago.
  • Panel type. OLED and AMOLED usually cost more than LCD; laptops vary by size, resolution and whether it's touchscreen.
  • Part options. Some models have more than one option. Whatever is available for your device, we show you at quoting time — you decide.
  • Related damage. A bent frame, a swollen battery or water damage adds to the work.
