Signs your battery needs replacing

These are the problems customers describe most often when they walk in:

  • Battery drains fast — 100% in the morning, hunting for a charger before noon.
  • The phone shuts down on its own even when it still shows 30% or 40%.
  • Gets hot — while charging, or even during normal use.
  • Charges slowly, or creeps up a few percent even after being plugged in for ages.
  • Swollen battery — the back cover lifting, the screen pushing out, or a laptop that no longer sits flat on the table.
  • Battery health on your iPhone has dropped badly, or Windows shows the "consider replacing your battery" warning on a laptop.

If you see a swollen battery, please stop using the device and bring it in soon. A swollen battery isn't something to sit on — WhatsApp us at 010-533 9970 with a photo and we'll tell you whether it's safe to keep using for now, or whether it should come in today.
