Masalah yang pelanggan selalu beritahu kami

Anda tak perlu tahu nama part yang rosak. Cukup cerita apa yang jadi:

  • Laptop slow: lambat boot, hang bila buka banyak tab, loading tak habis-habis.
  • Tak boleh on langsung: lampu tak menyala, atau on kejap terus mati.
  • Skrin blank, berkelip atau ada garis walaupun kipas jalan macam biasa.
  • Panas dan bising: badan laptop panas, kipas kuat, laptop pun jadi makin slow.
  • Bateri cepat habis atau kena cucuk charger sepanjang masa.
  • Port charge longgar: kena goyang wayar dulu baru masuk charge.
  • Kena air: tumpah kopi atau air atas keyboard.
  • Windows rosak, kena virus, atau fail penting hilang.

Problems customers usually tell us about

You don't need to know the name of the faulty part. Just tell us what's happening:

  • Laptop is slow: slow to boot, hangs with a few tabs open, loading that never ends.
  • Won't turn on at all: no lights, or it powers on briefly then dies.
  • Blank, flickering or lined screen even though the fan is running as usual.
  • Hot and noisy: the body heats up, the fan runs hard, and the laptop gets slower.
  • Battery drains fast or it only works plugged into the charger.
  • Loose charging port: you have to wiggle the cable before it charges.
  • Liquid damage: coffee or water spilled on the keyboard.
  • Broken Windows, a virus, or important files gone missing.
