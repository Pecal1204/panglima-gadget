Jatuh dalam sinki, kena hujan lebat masa naik motor, tertinggal dalam poket seluar yang terus masuk mesin basuh — telefon masuk air memang boleh berlaku kepada sesiapa sahaja. Sebelum apa-apa: jangan charge, jangan cuba on balik. Air yang bercampur dengan arus elektrik itulah yang selalunya rosakkan board — bukan air itu sendiri. Matikan phone terus, lap bahagian luar, jangan goncang, dan bawa masuk secepat mungkin. Beras pun tak membantu — ia langsung tak tarik air yang dah masuk dalam board.

Tanda-tanda yang pelanggan selalu cerita

  • Skrin ada tompok air, garis, warna pelik — atau terus gelap
  • Phone tak on langsung, atau on sekejap kemudian mati sendiri
  • Tak boleh charge, atau port charge panas dan nampak berkarat
  • Speaker bunyi pecah atau perlahan, mic tak masuk, orang sebelah sana tak dengar suara kita
  • Skrin tekan sendiri (ghost touch) atau touch tak jalan sebelah
  • Bateri cepat habis, atau phone panas melampau
  • Camera berembun, gambar keluar kabur
  • Laptop: keyboard melekat atau tak fungsi, kipas bunyi kuat, atau langsung tak on

Ramai pelanggan datang lambat sebab phone nampak elok pada hari pertama. Masalahnya karat merebak diam-diam — seminggu dua kemudian barulah mula rosak satu-satu. Kalau device anda dah kena air, bawa datang kedai walaupun ia nampak masih boleh guna.

  • Periksa board dengan teliti — cari kesan air, karat dan komponen terbakar
  • Basuh board dengan proses pembersihan khas untuk buang sisa air dan karat
  • Ganti komponen yang dah rosak, termasuk kerja board-level kalau perlu
  • Test penuh sebelum serah: charge, skrin, touch, camera, speaker, mic dan signal
  • Kalau board memang tak boleh diselamatkan, kami fokus pada pemulihan data dan gambar

Dropped in the sink, caught in heavy rain on the motorbike, left in a pocket that went straight into the wash — water damage happens to anyone. At Panglima Gadget we handle wet phones and laptops Monday to Saturday, for customers across Telok Panglima Garang, Banting, Jenjarom, Klang and around Kuala Langat.

First things first: do not charge it, and do not try to power it back on. Water mixed with electrical current is what usually damages the board — not the water alone. Switch the phone off, wipe the outside, don't shake it, and get it to us as soon as you can. Rice doesn't help either — it can't pull out water that is already inside the board.

Symptoms customers usually describe

  • Water marks, lines or odd colours on the screen — or a completely dark display
  • Device won't power on, or turns on briefly then dies
  • Won't charge, or the charging port runs hot and looks corroded
  • Speaker crackling or too quiet, dead mic, the person on the other end can't hear you
  • Ghost touch, or the touchscreen not responding on one side
  • Battery draining fast, or the phone getting extremely hot
  • Condensation in the camera, photos coming out blurry
  • Laptop: sticky or dead keys, loud fan, or nothing on power-up

A lot of customers come in late because the phone looked fine on day one. The problem is that corrosion spreads quietly — a week or two later, things start failing one by one. If your device has been wet, bring it to the shop even if it still seems usable.

What we do when we repair a water-damaged phone

  • Open the device and disconnect the battery first to stop current flowing
  • Inspect the board carefully for water tracks, corrosion and burnt components
  • Wash the board with a proper cleaning process to remove residue and corrosion
  • Replace damaged components, including board-level work where it's needed
  • Full testing before handover: charging, display, touch, camera, speaker, mic and signal
  • If the board genuinely can't be saved, we shift focus to recovering your data and photos

What determines the price

  • How long the device was submerged, and how long it sat before being brought to the shop
  • The type of liquid — clean tap water behaves very differently compared to seawater, drain water or sugary drinks
  • Whether the phone was switched on or charged after it got wet
  • How far corrosion has spread across the board
  • Which components need replacing — screen, battery, charging port, or ICs on the board
  • The device model and what parts cost for that model
