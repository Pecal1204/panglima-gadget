Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Google reviews widget

Our homepage loads a third-party RevuKit widget to display public Google Business Profile ratings and reviews. This may include a reviewer's public display name, profile photo, rating, review text, publication time, and links to Google Maps. Panglima Gadget does not use this public review data to identify or contact reviewers.

When the widget loads, your browser connects to cdn.revukit.com. RevuKit may receive standard connection information such as your IP address, browser details, requested page, and time of access, may use your browser's local storage to cache widget data, and may also cache public review content in its systems to operate the service. See the RevuKit Privacy Policy and RevuKit Terms. Google review content is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms.

Website data

We do not operate customer accounts or an enquiry form on this website. Our hosting and security provider may process standard technical information—such as IP address, browser type, requested page, time, and security events—to deliver and protect the site.

The site uses local browser storage to remember that you have already seen the one-time rebrand announcement. We do not use that setting for advertising or cross-site tracking.

External services

The RevuKit widget and links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, TikTok, Facebook, and Waze connect you to services operated by those companies. Their own terms and privacy policies apply after you open them.
